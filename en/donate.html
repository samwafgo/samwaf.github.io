Support Us ❤️

SamWaf is a completely open-source and free web application firewall. All code is released under the Apache 2.0 license, and every feature is available to everyone — no donation required.

If SamWaf has helped you, consider buying the author a coffee ☕. Your support goes toward:

  • Infrastructure costs: servers, domains and update sources
  • Test devices and compatibility verification
  • Subscription and usage costs for AI-assisted development tools
  • Ongoing feature development and maintenance

Every contribution is also a source of encouragement, helping the author stay motivated to keep developing and maintaining SamWaf.

Completely Voluntary

Donations are entirely voluntary, of any amount. All SamWaf features are equally available to everyone — there will never be "donation-locked" features.

Other Ways to Support

You can help the project without donating:

  • Give us a Star ⭐ on GitHub
  • Recommend SamWaf to your friends
  • Submit issues and PRs to make the product better together
